# Company AI Usage Policy (read by Gemma 4 on this laptop)

Never send any of the following to an external AI tool:

- Client or customer company names (for example "Acme Corp", "Aarav Logistics Pvt Ltd").
- Deal values, revenue, prices, salaries or any other money amounts (for example "₹4.2 Cr", "₹14,50,000").
- Internal or unreleased project codenames (for example "Project Falcon", "Project Trishul").
- Names, emails or phone numbers of employees, clients or customers.
- Credentials of any kind: passwords, API keys, tokens, secret keys.
- Purchase order numbers, invoice numbers and internal document IDs (for example "PO-2026-8891").
- Internal source code longer than a few lines.

These are fine to send:

- Public product names, public company names that are not our clients, general industry facts.
- Generic questions about writing, coding concepts or summarising, once the items above are removed.
